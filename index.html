Frequently asked questions

Is StudeCo free?

Yes. Core features are free for students. Storage limits are managed by platform administrators.

Who can join?

Secondary school and university students. You select your education level during onboarding.

How is my data protected?

We use industry-standard authentication (Supabase Auth), Row Level Security policies, and never store passwords in plain form. Private messages are only accessible to conversation participants.

Can I upload study materials?

Yes — PDFs, images, presentations and notes are supported, subject to administrator-configured limits and permitted formats. Copyrighted material should only be shared where you have the right to do so.

What happens if storage is full?

Administrators control upload availability. When capacity is reached, a clear message is shown and optional approved alternative destinations may be provided.
